Understand the review boundary

Signatures bind the root, role, decision and trusted policy hash. Signatures do not establish the human owner of a key. Demo keys represent a local protocol simulation; genuine independent review requires separately held keys. The v2 bundle is not automatically anchored on a public blockchain.
